?Each planet is a project and circles the sun, Mission Control itself, on its ring, the inner rings faster (the innermost takes 45 seconds). Point at a planet or the sun for its card, with its run buttons and a link to its page; the card stays put so you can reach them. Point at any planet and every planet pauses, then they stay still for 3 seconds after you leave and ease back to speed. Red means it needs you. A ring means uncommitted changes, a moon means a server is running.

Arrange: drag a planet to move it, it snaps to the nearest orbit, and a new outer orbit appears when you drag past the last one. Escape cancels a drag.

Left drag on empty space to pan, right drag to turn and tilt, scroll to zoom, double click to reset, click a planet to open it.
